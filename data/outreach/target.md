# Ideal customer profile

- Decision makers at SMBs and mid-market companies who need software, websites, VoIP, or IT infrastructure
- Founders / COOs / IT managers of companies with 5–200 staff
- Agencies and studios that need Unity game development or custom web apps
- Businesses replacing landlines with VoIP / SIP / softphones
- Companies needing RDP, VPS, server setup, or network fixes

Prefer English or Urdu-speaking markets we can serve from Pakistan (local + remote international). Avoid pure consumer apps with no budget signal.
